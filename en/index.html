An invitation

We got into the habit of telling each other about our days. Even the days when nothing happened turned into something to tell. After sharing our days apart for so long, we decided to start having the same ones.

On October 25th, in a hanok in Yangpyeong, we are getting married. It is also the day our two families, who have lived a long way apart, finally sit down at the same table.

This invitation goes to the people who have been beside us. We are living abroad at the moment, so we cannot put it in your hands ourselves, and we hope you will forgive us for reaching you through a screen.

Nothing would make us happier than seeing your face that day and getting to talk with you. And if you cannot make it, the thought alone is more than enough.

October 25th, in Yangpyeong. It is a bit of a journey, and we would be glad beyond words if you made it with us.

Wonkyo, son of Sukyun Choe and Miae Lee
Riley, daughter of Lance Calicchia and Kelly Calicchia

By car
Search any Korean navigation app for the venue name, or use the buttons above. From southeast Seoul it is about 50 minutes to an hour via Olympic-daero and the Misa interchange onto National Route 6.
Parking
The hall has its own lot and parking is free. Only one wedding is held at a time, so there is plenty of room. Driving is the easy option.
By train
KTX Gangneung line to Yangpyeong Station, then 10 to 15 minutes by taxi from the station exit. Yangpyeong is 30 minutes from Seoul Station and 20 from Cheongnyangni.
